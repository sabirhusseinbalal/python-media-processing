30 Short Stories with Moral
(10 Line Short Stories)

Story 1: The Honest Boy
Tom found a wallet on the street.
He opened it and saw money.
He looked around but saw no one.
He felt confused for a moment.
Then he decided to help.
He went to a nearby shop.
The shopkeeper knew the owner.
Tom returned the wallet.
The man was very happy.
Tom felt proud too.
Moral: Honesty is always the best choice.
Vocabulary: wallet, owner, return, proud, street
Story 2: The Lazy Student
Anna had an exam the next day.
She did not study at all.
She watched TV all night.
Her friend warned her.
But she did not listen.
The exam was very hard.
She could not answer much.
She felt stressed and sad.
She started studying later.
She changed her habits.
Moral: Don’t wait until it’s too late.
Vocabulary: exam, study, answer, habit, stress
